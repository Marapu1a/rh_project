NOTHING UP OUR SLEEVES.
Which purchases earn tickets?+

Only supported PAIR Infinity purchases paid in USDG, with a verified buyer and token recipient. Each 100 USDG of eligible net spending earns one ticket for each draw type. Transfers, holding and sales do not create tickets. Selling does not cancel tickets already earned. Supported routes and verified contract addresses will be published before launch.

When does a Short draw happen?+

No earlier than 6 hours after the previous Short finishes, and only when the system is ready. The initial prize basket starts at 100 USDG. Up to 10 wallets can win, with at most one prize per wallet. Unawarded funds stay in the Short reserve. Participating Short tickets are consumed when the draw finishes; Monthly tickets remain separate.

How do tickets affect my chances?+

For Short, e tickets give a random admission chance of 0.8 × e / (e + 1): 40% with one ticket, approaching 80% with many. Admission is not a win. If more than 10 wallets pass, 10 winners are selected equally among them. Multiple wallets can have an advantage; we do not claim one wallet equals one person.

What makes the monthly jackpot different?+

No more often than once a month. A single draw has a 75% chance of awarding the frozen current jackpot to one winner, and a 25% chance of rolling it over. Winner weights use e / (e + 1). Readiness requires at least 100 USDG in the current jackpot and the next 100 USDG starting reserve funded.

Where do prizes come from?+

The launch profile uses a 3% creator fee. Received creator revenue is split 90% for prizes, 5% for operating costs and 5% for the team. External prize funding creates no project fee. Frozen prizes and assigned rewards cannot fund operations. Assigned rewards are paid automatically where possible; a claim path remains available if payment is delayed.
